### First public release

Fourteen explorable cosmic events in a single offline HTML file:

- **Theia, the birth of the Moon** and **Last morning of the Cretaceous** (Chicxulub), each from the ground, from space and from the incoming body.
- **The comet against Jupiter** (Shoemaker-Levy 9), **The morning of Chelyabinsk**, **The forge of gold** (GW170817, with the gravitational wave made audible).
- Three black-hole scenes with curved light rays: **Cygnus X-1**, **The star torn apart**, **Falling into a black hole**.
- **The night Betelgeuse explodes**, **The ring of Mars** (Phobos), **Milky Way against Andromeda** (27,000 test stars integrated live, two scenarios), **The death of the Sun**.
- Two clearly labelled fictions, eleven bodies integrated step by step: **What if a black hole dislodged Saturn?** (Saturn falls sunward and brushes past Earth, two scenarios) and **What if a black hole carried off the Sun?** (the Sun is torn away from its planets, three scenarios for Earth).
- In every scene, a “Center on…” list of points of interest and arrows that show where they are.

Time from 1/10,000 speed to hundreds of millions of years per second, an automatic tempo that slows down at key moments, a journal that explains each step, and 11 languages (the simulator follows your browser's language, English otherwise).

Download `cosmic-events-1.0.0.html` below and double-click it, or [open it online](https://intelligentrebel.github.io/cosmic-events/).
