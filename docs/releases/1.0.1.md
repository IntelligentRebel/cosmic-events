### Fix: Milky Way against Andromeda no longer jumps back in time

On screens refreshing at 60 images per second or more, the Andromeda scene kept jumping back by up to half a billion years around the collision and after it, then replaying the same stretch. The orbit calculation was allowed to run slightly ahead of the clock, and the scene mistook that for a jump back in the timeline. It now always stays just behind the clock.

Download `cosmic-events-1.0.1.html` below and double-click it, or [open it online](https://intelligentrebel.github.io/cosmic-events/).
